<h2>What Is B.Pharm?</h2>
<p class="isSelectedEnd"><strong>B.Pharm</strong>, or Bachelor of Pharmacy, is a four-year undergraduate degree that introduces students to pharmaceutical sciences and the healthcare industry.</p>
<p class="isSelectedEnd">The course generally covers areas such as:</p>
<ul data-spread="false">
<li>Pharmaceutics</li>
<li>Pharmaceutical Chemistry</li>
<li>Pharmacology</li>
<li>Pharmacognosy</li>
<li>Pharmaceutical Analysis</li>
<li>Medicinal Chemistry</li>
<li>Microbiology</li>
<li>Clinical Pharmacy</li>
<li>Drug Regulatory Affairs</li>
<li>Pharmacokinetics</li>
</ul>
<p class="isSelectedEnd">A good B.Pharm program should combine classroom learning with laboratory work, practical training, internships, and exposure to the pharmaceutical industry.</p>
<p class="isSelectedEnd">For example, Jigyasa University's B.Pharm program is structured as a <strong>4-year/8-semester course</strong> and lists 10+2 with PCB or PCM among its eligibility requirements.</p>
